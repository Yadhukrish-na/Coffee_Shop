# ☕ Brew Ghar - Indian Coffee Shop

A beautifully designed responsive website for an Indian coffee shop featuring South Indian filter coffee, masala chai, and house-made treats.

## Features

- **Indian Coffee Culture**: Authentic South Indian filter coffee and chai experience
- **Smooth Animations**: Fade-up entrance animations and hover effects
- **Gallery**: 6 high-quality photos with caption overlays
- **Rich Menu**: 10+ items with prices in Indian rupees
- **Responsive Design**: Mobile-friendly layout
- **Modern UI**: Warm, inviting color palette with smooth transitions

## Pages & Sections

- **Hero Section**: Welcoming introduction with call-to-action buttons
- **About**: Story of Brew Ghar and coffee culture
- **Specialties**: What makes us special
- **Menu**: Full drink and food menu
- **Gallery**: Visual showcase with animated captions
- **Contact**: Location, hours, and contact form

## Tech Stack

- HTML5
- CSS3 (with animations and transitions)
- Vanilla JavaScript (smooth scroll navigation)
