Our story

Heritage coffee, chai, and warm community

At Brew Ghar, we blend the soul of Indian coffee culture with a friendly neighbourhood cafe. Our beans come from Coorg and Araku Valley, our chai is steeped with cardamom and ginger, and our space is made for quiet mornings or lively adda with friends.

Come for the filter kaapi served the traditional way, stay for the aromatic chai, and enjoy house-made snacks with a modern twist.
